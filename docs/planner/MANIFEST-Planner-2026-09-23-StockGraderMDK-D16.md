## The load-bearing item

**§2.1.** A provenance row describes a fetch — its URL, when it happened, and the
hash of what came back. Once a fetch has happened unrecorded, that record cannot
be reconstructed from anything, because re-fetching produces a new fetch rather
than evidence of the old one.

The fact slice is where the volume arrives. In the other order, the largest body
of data this project holds becomes the part that can never be traced.

cheap now, impossible later.
